# Support GTA4NX

GTA4NX is a free, non-commercial community project. The port and its public releases remain
available without requiring payment.

## Financial support

If you would like to support the development voluntarily, use PayPal's normal **Send Money** option
and enter the recipient email below:

**[Open PayPal Send Money](https://www.paypal.com/pt/digital-wallet/send-receive-money/send-money)**

Recipient: `kabbapaypal@gmail.com`

Any money received will be reinvested in development costs and in future projects requested by the
community. A contribution does not purchase a feature, provide access to game files or guarantee a
release date.

## Technical support and project requests

For problems with GTA4NX, open a GitHub issue and include the exact build, what you were doing and
the relevant logs. Do not upload copyrighted game data.

The community may also use GitHub issues to suggest future projects. Suggestions will be considered,
but acceptance and delivery dates cannot be guaranteed.
